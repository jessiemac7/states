OGUN STATE

Slogan: Gateway State

Its creation stem from the old Western State in 1976, it economy is dominated by agriculture
and industrial activites with various investments in cocoa and digital innovation.

The slogan for Ogun state is called "Gateway State".
The state was created by the military government of General Murtala Mohammed on February 3, 1976.

The Recent development in Ogun state is the establishment of a cocoa enclave and a
$1.2 billion foreign direct investments for job creation through battery recycling technology
